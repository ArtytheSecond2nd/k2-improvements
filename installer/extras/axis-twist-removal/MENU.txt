Removes the retired Axis Twist extra; does not calibrate or move the printer.

Before continuing: printer idle, SAVE_CONFIG completed, Klipper ready.

The uninstaller backs up ATC files/calibration and restores stock probe.py
using the verified PR Touch wrapper hash (1.1.3.13, 1.1.5.2, or 1.1.5.5).
Unknown hashes stop with a reflash instruction; no helper is guessed.

Restores PR Touch registration, removes ATC includes/module/config links and
saved calibration, and keeps global/material Z offsets and saved meshes.
On Cartographer, keeps probe XY offsets enabled.

Finishes with the protected Klippy reload, firmware reset, and motor-ready wait.
Re-mesh and recheck Z offsets afterward. Keep the backup if recovery is needed.
